# AreaJam User Manual

**Find your tribe. Match with musicians today.**

Welcome to the AreaJam handbook for **[areajam.com](https://areajam.com/)**. AreaJam helps local musicians discover nearby players through clip-driven swipe decks, open local directories, shareable artist profile pages, and a direct match-and-message inbox.

## 1. Quick Start (No Account Needed)

You can explore AreaJam immediately without signing in:

1. Open **[areajam.com](https://areajam.com/)**. You start on the **Find your tribe. Match with musicians today.** carousel showing players in your current metro board.
2. **Jump straight into swiping:**
   - **On Mobile:** Tap **Start swiping** (or tap any card in the slow carousel) to launch the full-screen swipe deck. You can also jump between **Swipe**, **Browse**, **Messages**, and **Profile** using the floating 4-tab bottom dock.
   - **On Desktop:** Click any player card in the carousel to jump directly to their card in the 2-column swipe view, or click **Browse musicians** to scan everyone at once.
3. **Switch themes anytime:** Click the **Sun / Moon** icon button in the top-right header to toggle between Dark and Light mode.
4. **Need a refresher in-app?** Click the **?** (How AreaJam works) icon button in the top-right header.

## 2. Picking Your City or Starting a Local Board

AreaJam organizes players by local metro boards, while still letting you discover nearby cities if a brand-new town board is just getting started.

Open the **City Picker** anytime:
- **Mobile:** Tap the **left half (City name)** of the integrated capsule in the top bar.
- **Desktop:** Click the **Location pill** (for example `Nashville, 615`) in the top-right header, or click the city pill above the landing headline.

Inside **Pick your city or state**, you can:

- **Search:** Filter featured cities, all 50 US states & DC, and area codes in real time.
- **Auto-detect (`Use my current city`):** Tap **Use my current city** to jump to your metro board via optional IP geolocation lookup (or click **Allow** in the desktop top prompt when it first appears).
- **Show other cities after local cards:** Check this option if you want cards from neighboring cities to continue seamlessly once your home city's cards finish.
- **Show sample preview profiles:** Toggle **demo sample profiles** on or off. Fictional sample musicians carry a `Fictional sample` badge so you can preview how clips, gear lockers, and swipe animations feel even before live players post in an area.
- **Start a board in your town:** Don't see your city listed? Fill out **City name**, optional **State**, and optional 3-digit **Area code**, then click **Open board for this city** to create and switch to a local board immediately.

> **Tip:** When a city has no live players yet and AreaJam falls back to nearby cities nationwide, desktop displays a notice above the card with rough distance badges (`~770 mi`). On mobile, that extra banner stays hidden so your swipe card keeps full vertical screen space.

## 3. Swiping & Filtering Musicians

AreaJam adapts its layout between phones and wider screens while syncing all filters seamlessly.

### Mobile Single-Bar Layout & Integrated Filter

On phones, AreaJam keeps the top chrome to **one compact header bar** so the swipe card fills the screen cleanly:
- **Left side of the center capsule:** Opens **Pick your city or state**.
- **Right side of the center capsule:** Opens the **Player Type dropdown** (`All players`, `Drummers`, `Guitarists`, `Bassists`, `Vocalists`, `Keys & Synth`, `Horns & Strings`, `Producers`, or `Songwriters`). Picking a role immediately filters your deck.

### Touch Gestures & the 5 Action Orbs

On both mobile and desktop swipe cards, you can drag a card **right to Jam** or **left to Pass**, or use the 5 circular action buttons underneath the card:

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Rewind / Undo** | Restores the most recently passed or swiped card |
| **Pass (`X`)** | Skips the current musician and advances the deck |
| **Quick Info (`i`)** | Opens a compact dialog with bio, availability, genres, and gear summary |
| **Jam (Flame)** | Likes & opens the **Jam Invite** composer (or opens chat if already matched) |
| **Expand Profile** | Opens the musician's full Artist Profile page |

### Desktop Filters & Keyboard Shortcuts

On wider screens (`>= 768px`), the left column offers combined search (by name, gear, genre, or neighborhood), a **Genre** dropdown, and multi-select instrument/role pills (`Drummers`, `Guitarists`, `Bassists`, etc.).

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `ArrowLeft` (`←`) | Pass on current card |
| `ArrowRight` (`→`) | Jam / send invite on current card |
| `ArrowUp` (`↑`) | Open full Artist Profile page |
| `Space` or `M` | Toggle clip audio mute / unmute |
| `Z` | Undo last swipe |

## 4. Browsing the Open Directory

Switch to **Browse players** (top nav on desktop, **Browse** tab on the mobile dock) when you want a grid overview instead of one card at a time:

- Filter instantly with the search bar (matches titles, subtitles, neighborhoods, genres, seeking notes, and gear locker items).
- Click any card's media area or title to open its full Artist Profile page, use the card's play control to audition clips in-place, or click **Open clip reels** to return to the swipe deck.
- Toggle **Samples** in the desktop header (or inside **Pick your city or state** on mobile) to filter between live community listings and demo cards.

## 5. Artist Profile Pages & Gear Lockers

Every live or sample musician has a full **Artist Profile page** (and a direct shareable link via **Share link** or `?p=<profile-id>`):

Each profile includes:
- **Media & Clip Showcase:** Play linked YouTube videos/Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikToks, Vimeo, SoundCloud tracks, or direct MP4/MP3 clips. Use the card's top **Tap for sound** control to toggle audio.
- **What I'm looking for:** Instruments, rehearsal availability, genres, neighborhood, and session goals.
- **Gear locker & rig:** Structured rig list organized by category (instruments, amps, pedals, mics, backline, etc.).
- **Local scene neighbors & Wall Notes:** View nearby musicians in the same city board, and read or post public **Wall Notes** (signing in is required to post or delete your own notes).
- **Owner Customization (`Customize page`):** When viewing a live profile you created, click **Customize page** to switch page themes (such as **Pink Signature**, **Midnight Vinyl**, **Tube Amber**, and **Emerald Club**), update your status line, add **"Sounds like"** influences, and add or remove Gear Locker items.

## 6. Building & Publishing Your Profile (`Profile & studio`)

You can draft your musician profile freely before ever signing in. Open **Profile & studio** (top nav on desktop, **Profile** tab on mobile) to walk through the 3-step builder:

1. **Step 1 — Import clips:**
   - Enter your public **YouTube `@handle`** or channel URL and click **Load videos** to browse your videos and Shorts and auto-fill stage details.
   - Or paste individual links from **Instagram Reels**, **YouTube**, **TikTok**, **Vimeo**, **SoundCloud**, or HTTPS MP4/MP3 media, plus your optional `@instagram` handle.
   - Click the **Star** button on your best clip so it plays first automatically on your swipe card and profile page.
2. **Step 2 — Instruments & vibe:**
   - Set your stage/project title, role subtitle, neighborhood, city board, primary instruments, genres, what you're looking to jam on, bio, availability, and optional Gear Locker items.
3. **Step 3 — Preview & publish:**
   - Inspect your live swipe card preview side-by-side (`Profile details verified for Firestore`).
   - Sign in with **Google** (or **Apple** when enabled) to publish your profile live to Cloud Firestore.
   - From **Profile & studio**, you can also view or edit your published profiles, delete a listing you own, inspect your **Blocked accounts** list, and **Unblock** anyone anytime.

## 7. Jam Invites, Matches & Messages Inbox

Swiping right (or clicking the **Flame / Invite to jam** button) on a live community profile lets you connect directly without giving out personal contact details upfront:

1. **Send a Jam Invite:** Write a brief custom note (or tap one of the quick starter prompts like *"Watched your clip — down to grab a rehearsal hour this week?"*) and click **Send jam invite**.
2. **Manage Folders in `Messages`:**
   - **New Matches Rail:** Pending incoming requests appear highlighted with a flame badge at the top alongside your connected matches for one-tap switching.
   - **`Inbox`:** Active 1-on-1 conversations for every accepted match. Once an invite is accepted by either person, you can message back and forth freely in real time—complete with one-tap starter phrases and `Enter` to send.
   - **`Requests`:** Incoming jam invitations from other musicians. Review their note and profile, then click **Accept** (opens the match chat immediately) or **Decline**.
   - **`Sent`:** Outgoing invites waiting on a response, with a **Cancel** option if you change your mind.

## 8. Safety, Blocking & Reporting

Safety controls are built into profiles, jam invites, and every message thread:

- **Block (`Block` button):** Immediately hides that user's profiles, wall notes, pending jam invites, and message threads from your views, and Firestore Security Rules enforce blocking server-side so neither account can send new invites or messages to the other. You can review and unblock users anytime in **Profile & studio**.
- **Report (`Report` button):** Flag profiles, wall notes, or message threads under structured categories (*Spam or promo links*, *Harassment or abusive messages*, *Impersonation or fake musician profile*, *Inappropriate clip or wall content*, or *Other safety concern*) with optional details.
- **Location Privacy:** AreaJam never tracks your browser GPS coordinates. City auto-detection is strictly opt-in using coarse IP-to-city matching only when you click **Allow** or **Use my current city**.

## 9. Installing AreaJam on Your Phone (PWA)

AreaJam works cleanly as an installable home-screen web app:

- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Open `areajam.com` in Safari, tap the **Share** button, and select **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** Open `areajam.com` in Chrome, open the browser menu (`⋮`), and tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## 10. FAQ & Troubleshooting

- **Why are video/audio clips muted by default?**
  Mobile and desktop browsers block unmuted autoplay before a gesture. Tap **Tap for sound** on any card (or press `Space` / `M` on desktop) to unmute playback.
- **Why do some cards say `Fictional sample`?**
  Demo sample profiles let you audition AreaJam's player filters, cards, and gear lockers right away. Jam invites unlock only on live community musician profiles signed in with Auth. To view live boards only, toggle **Samples** off in the desktop header or inside **Pick your city or state** on mobile.
- **Why does my phone header look different from desktop?**
  On mobile (`< 768px`), AreaJam merges Location and Player Type filtering into a single top capsule and hides desktop fallback notices so your swipe card has maximum height. Switch back to desktop or open **Browse** for multi-instrument bubble filters and search.
- **How do I update to the latest deployed version if a PWA cache feels stale?**
  Hard-refresh the tab or close and reopen the installed PWA—AreaJam's service worker automatically activates new builds and removes older shell caches on load.
- **Which sign-in methods work right now?**
  **Continue with Google** is active live on `areajam.com`. **Continue with Apple** becomes active once Apple Developer Services ID, Team ID, Key ID, and Private Key credentials are configured in Firebase Console.
