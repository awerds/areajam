Complete User & Creator Manual

AreaJam is a clip-first web platform for discovering local bandmates, booking working local music teachers, and hiring remote-friendly session players—with zero app-store download and zero middleman commission.

1 Getting Started

You can browse musicians, watch performance clips, inspect gear lockers, and switch between US cities immediately at areajam.com—no account required. Sign in with Google or Apple only when you want to publish a profile, send a direct Jam/Session/Lesson request, leave a Scene Wall note, or chat in your Inbox.

  • City Boards: Click the city pill in the top bar (e.g., Los Angeles, 213) to switch between 50+ US metros, opt into IP city detection, or create a custom board for your own hometown.
  • Light & Dark Themes: Toggle the sun/moon icon in the header anytime. AreaJam remembers your preference automatically.
  • Install as a Mobile Web App (PWA): On iOS Safari or Android Chrome, tap Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen native-like experience.

2 Finding Musicians, Session Players & Teachers

Teacher and Session Player roles live directly as optional badges on a musician's primary profile. Use the three discovery tabs in the top navigation bar (or the compact segmented switcher at the top of mobile Browse & Swipe) to filter instantly:

Discovery View Who Appears Key Details Shown
Find a Musician Every player in your selected city Neighborhood, instruments, clips, genres, and optional Session/Teacher badges.
Find a Session Player Musicians with offersSession enabled Amber Session · $rate badge (e.g., $85/track), studio rig, and remote availability.
Find a Teacher Musicians with offersLessons enabled Emerald Teacher · $rate badge (e.g., $70/hr), teaching focus, and weekly availability.

Browse Grid vs. Clip Reels (Swipe Deck)

Use Browse to scan an entire city at once by instrument, genre, or keyword (search matches names, neighborhoods, genres, gear items, and service notes). Tap Open clip reels (or Swipe on mobile) to audition players one vertical card at a time with swipe gestures or keyboard arrows.

3 Local-First & Remote-Friendly Discovery

AreaJam always prioritizes musicians in your selected city first. Inside any of the three discovery views (Musicians, Session Players, or Teachers), toggle Include remote-friendly ON to expand your pool:

  • Strict Local-First Ordering: Every matching local player in your city appears first, followed strictly afterward by remote-friendly musicians from other cities.
  • Clear Remote Badges: Out-of-area remote matches are tagged with a blue Remote badge and their home city (e.g., Los Angeles, CA).
  • Ideal for Remote Studio Work: Recording an EP in Nashville or Austin and need vintage Rhodes stems or cello overdubs? Switch to Find a Session Player and toggle Include remote-friendly to reach studio pros nationwide.

4 Contextual Connect, Following & Messages

Swiping right or tapping Connect on any card or profile opens the contextual Connect dialog so the other musician immediately knows why you're reaching out:

Tier When Available How It Works
Follow Always (bottom tier) 1-tap bookmark—no note or approval required. Saves the musician to Messages → Following.
Jam Always Sends a jam invite with a short note + quick-tap starter prompts.
Session When musician offers session work Displays their session rate + sends a tagged Session request.
Lesson When musician offers lessons Displays their lesson rate + sends a tagged Lesson request.
Smart Defaults: Swiping right inside Find a Session Player automatically pre-selects Session; inside Find a Teacher automatically pre-selects Lesson; inside Find a Musician defaults to Jam (or Follow on showcase cards).

Messages Workspace

  • Inbox: Direct 1-on-1 chat threads with musicians who accepted your request (or whose request you accepted).
  • Requests: Incoming requests tagged with Jam, Session, or Lesson badges so you can review the note and accept or decline in one tap.
  • Sent: Pending outgoing requests with instant cancel if plans change.
  • Following: Every musician you've followed, with quick links to view their profile or upgrade to a direct request.

5 Building Your Profile in 3 Steps (YouTube Import)

Open Profile & studio in the navigation bar to build your draft before signing in. You can import clips directly from YouTube or paste links from Instagram Reels, TikTok, Vimeo, SoundCloud, or direct HTTPS MP4/MP3 URLs.

Step 1: Import Clips

Enter your YouTube @handle or channel URL to load your public videos and Shorts, tap up to 4 thumbnails, and auto-fill your stage name and bio.

Step 2: Instruments, Vibe & Services

Pick up to 6 instruments and 4 genres, add your neighborhood and availability, and optionally enable free Session Player, Teacher, and Remote-friendly badges with rates and service notes.

Step 3: Preview & Publish

Inspect your live card preview (including pricing badges), confirm you are 18+ and agree to Terms/Privacy, sign in with Google or Apple, and go live instantly.

6 Managing Your Artist Page

Every published profile has a dedicated, shareable URL (areajam.com/?area=la&mode=jam&profile=your-id) featuring a sticky 9:16 video stage, identity header, bio/influences, Lessons & session work card, Gear locker & rig, unified public Followers list, and Scene wall.

  • Customize Page (Owner Only): Click Customize page on your own profile to switch color themes (6 palettes), update your mood/status banner, edit influences, or toggle your Session Player / Teacher / Remote-friendly badges and pricing anytime.
  • Gear Locker: Add instruments, amps, pedals, mics, or backline pieces categorized by type so potential bandmates and session clients know your exact rig.
  • Unified Public Followers: All public connections appear cleanly under Followers—student and client relationship labels are never exposed publicly.

7 Trust, Safety & Privacy

  • Mutual Consent Messaging: No unsolicited DMs—direct chat only opens after a request is explicitly accepted.
  • Block & Report: Every profile and chat thread includes one-tap Report and Block controls. Blocking immediately hides that user's profiles, invites, and messages.
  • Strict URL & Media Security: All embedded clips are strictly validated on both client and server against an HTTPS allowlist (YouTube, Instagram, Vimeo, TikTok, SoundCloud) and rendered inside sandboxed iframes.
  • Data Portability & Account Deletion: In Profile & studio, you can export your AreaJam data as JSON or permanently delete your account and associated Firestore records anytime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AreaJam take a cut of lesson or session fees?
No. Listing Teacher and Session Player badges, posting your rates, and connecting with students or studio clients is 100% free. You arrange payment directly with each other.

Do I need to upload large video files?
No—AreaJam embeds your existing YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Vimeo, or SoundCloud links so your clips stay fast and your profile takes under 60 seconds to build.
