Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 — Applies to areajam.com

Summary: Browse local players with zero account or ad trackers. We never publish your sign-in email address, never request GPS location, use IP-to-city matching only with your click consent, and let you export your data or permanently delete your account inside the app at any time.

1. Who We Are

AreaJam (areajam.com) is a regional music discovery and collaboration platform helping local musicians find players to jam with. Contact us regarding privacy or data requests at privacy@areajam.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Authentication Account Data: When you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Apple, Google Firebase Authentication provides your unique account ID (UID), display name, email address, and optional profile photo URL. Your sign-in email is kept private and is never written to public musician profile documents.
  • Public Profile & Community Content: When you create and publish a musician listing in Profile & studio, the fields you submit—such as stage title, instruments, city board, neighborhood name, genres, bio, availability, Gear Locker entries, linked YouTube/Instagram/TikTok/Vimeo/SoundCloud clip URLs, optional social handle, and public Wall Notes—become publicly visible to anyone browsing AreaJam so musicians can discover you.
  • Jam Invites & Direct Messages: When you send or accept a jam invite, Cloud Firestore stores your participant IDs, display names, invite note, and 1-on-1 thread messages strictly between the two participants. Messages are gated by participant rules and mutual block checks.
  • Safety & Moderation Data: If you block or report another user, AreaJam stores your private block list (under your UID) and write-only safety reports (target user, reason, optional note) to enforce blocks and review abuse.
  • Browser Storage (localStorage & Service Worker Cache): Without tracking pixels or third-party ad cookies, your browser locally stores your UI preferences (selected city board, theme preference, filters, passed-card history, optional sample visibility, and city-detection choice) and offline shell assets.

3. Location Data (No GPS)

AreaJam does not access browser or device GPS hardware and does not track precise coordinates. Only if you click Allow or Use my current city, your browser performs a coarse IP-to-city lookup via third-party lookup APIs (ipwho.is or fallback ipapi.co with no-referrer) solely to select the closest metro city board locally in your browser.

4. Third-Party Services & Embedded Media

  • Google Firebase (Auth & Cloud Firestore): Stores authentication sessions, public profiles, wall notes, invites, messages, blocks, and safety reports under strict Firestore Security Rules.
  • Linked Media Embeds & YouTube Data: Profiles may link external clips from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Vimeo, or SoundCloud. Playing embedded media connects directly to those providers under their respective privacy policies.
  • GitHub Pages Hosting: Serves static site files over HTTPS; GitHub may log standard web server request metadata for security and reliability.

5. Your Rights, Export & Account Deletion

You can exercise your privacy rights directly in the app or by emailing privacy@areajam.com (answered within 30 days):

  • Export Your Data: Signed-in users can click Profile & studio → Account, data & legal → Download my data (JSON) to download a portable copy of your account summary, published profiles, invites, threads, blocks, and preferences.
  • Delete Your Account: Signed-in users can permanently delete their account directly via Profile & studio → Account, data & legal → Delete account. After re-authentication if required, AreaJam deletes your published profiles, their wall notes, your block list, cancels pending invites you sent, clears local device preferences, and deletes your Firebase Auth user record.
  • California & EEA/UK Users: AreaJam does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may request access, correction, or deletion under applicable GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA rights.

6. Children & Minimum Age (18+)

AreaJam is intended strictly for adults aged 18 and older who are organizing real-world rehearsals and musical collaborations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account or profile, contact privacy@areajam.com for prompt removal.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as features evolve (including future native iOS builds). Material changes update the Effective Date above.
