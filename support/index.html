Blocking & Reporting Abuse (24-Hour Review)

If you encounter spam, harassment, impersonation, or inappropriate links:

  1. Open the musician's Profile page or your Messages thread.
  2. Click Block to immediately hide that account across AreaJam and enforce server-side invite/message blocking. You can review or unblock accounts anytime in Profile & studio.
  3. Click Report to submit a report directly to our moderation queue. Moderation reports are reviewed within 24 hours, with offending content removed and repeat or severe violators permanently removed.

Self-Service Data Export & Account Deletion

You control your data directly inside AreaJam without needing to email support:

  1. Open areajam.com and navigate to the Profile & studio tab.
  2. Scroll to the Account, data & legal section while signed in.
  3. Click Download my data (JSON) to export a local copy of your account data, or click Delete account and type DELETE to permanently erase your authentication account, published musician profiles, their wall notes, your block list, pending sent invites, and local device state.

If you no longer have access to your Google or Apple login and need manual profile removal, email privacy@areajam.com with your profile name and city board.

Rehearsal Meet-Up Tips

  • Chat in AreaJam Messages first to verify rehearsal goals, tempo, gear needs, and scheduling.
  • Book first jams at public commercial rehearsal studios, music shops, or open mics rather than private residences.
